## Strong source-backed additions to prepare

1. **Warbreaker / the Prophet / Vasher.** Author confirmation, not merely a fan identity theory. FanX 2026 entry 3 confirms the identity and corrects the visit chronology to before Warbreaker, during Vasher's travels involving Roshar and Sel. Keep the historical visit distinct from December's present-day events.
   - https://wob.coppermind.net/events/555-fanx-2026/ (September 25, entry 3; indexed transcript retrieved)
   - https://www.17thshard.com/forums/topic/203111-the-prophet/ (opened; distinguish the later confirmation from earlier speculation)
2. **Warbreaker / Breaths and purified power.** The binding's passphrases and discussion of power stripped of its original Intent deserve their own callout. Preserve the qualification that the power is no longer ordinary Breaths; do not label the whole mechanism ordinary Awakening without that distinction.
   - https://www.17thshard.com/forums/topic/203167-new-arcana-we-have-learned-discuss/ (opened; passage discussed toward end of thread; exact chapter for this passage not independently established)
3. **Elantris / AonDor / Aon Reo.** The Ever-Slaying Sword's Aon is both a magic connection and an illustration-specific Easter egg: Coppermind identifies Reo in Tran Nguyen's painting, while noting the exact Aon is not named in prose.
   - https://coppermind.net/wiki/Aon (indexed Trivia and chapter 26 citation)
   - https://coppermind.net/wiki/Megalith?mobileaction=toggle_view_mobile (indexed chapter XXVI reference)
4. **Mistborn / Hemalurgy-adjacent spiritweb pinning.** The silver spike pins the Megalith's Spiritual aspect. The forum specifically distinguishes this from confidently declaring silver an ordinary power-stealing Hemalurgic metal. Do not repeat the opening post's stronger assertion without its subsequent qualifications.
   - https://www.17thshard.com/forums/topic/203167-new-arcana-we-have-learned-discuss/ (opened; replies correcting the opening bullet list)
   - https://coppermind.net/wiki/Megalith?mobileaction=toggle_view_mobile (indexed chapter XXVI)
5. **Isles of the Emberdark / Shadows for Silence / the Evil.** The Megalith and smaller demons connect with the wider Eidolith/Threnodite-entity material. Coppermind's Evil article cites Fires XXIV and XLI alongside Emberdark 25, 27, 39 and Shadows for Silence. Explain the shared entity/lore family without equating every demon with the same named individual or a shade.
   - https://coppermind.net/wiki/Evil (indexed article and reference list)
6. **Stormlight / Valor.** Fires substantially expands a Shard already referenced in Rhythm of War chapter 25's epigraph. Medelantorius and Valor's temple/search are distinct from the older casual Shard-name mention.
   - https://coppermind.net/wiki/Valor?mobileaction=toggle_view_desktop (indexed identity and reference list)
   - https://www.17thshard.com/forums/topic/203128-valor-godsflame-and-ashyn/?comment=2118654&do=findComment (indexed quoted passages)
7. **Stormlight / Ashyn / Godsflame.** An explicit Ashyn reference appears in the discussion's cited passage. The connection is supported; claims that Valor caused Ashyn's catastrophe, or that Godsflame is definitely a particular Surge or disease, remain interpretations.
   - https://www.17thshard.com/forums/topic/203128-valor-godsflame-and-ashyn/?comment=2118654&do=findComment (indexed discussion)
8. **Tress / Shadows for Silence / silver's effects on Investiture.** Fires XXVI's silver behavior should be compared with the established spore/shade effects, preserving differences between systems rather than declaring silver universally blocks magic.
   - https://coppermind.net/wiki/Silver (indexed chapter XXVI reference)
   - https://www.17thshard.com/forums/topic/203167-new-arcana-we-have-learned-discuss/ (opened discussion of distinctions)

## Explicit lore and unresolved candidate links

- **Zuhel, Nahel and Luhel bonds:** Zuhel is documented; a neat one-bond-per-Realm diagram in the forum is an interpretation, not automatically a confirmed universal law. Compare with Stormlight and Tress without making all mechanisms identical.
  - https://www.17thshard.com/forums/topic/203104-zuhel-bond/ (opened)
  - https://coppermind.net/wiki/Witchcraft?mobileaction=toggle_view_mobile (indexed)
- **Hoid's Sixth Word / Fortune:** the book names a mechanism important to his wider appearances. Forum theories about the other Words, origin world and Dawnshards must remain theories. Coppermind's Invested Art list retains unknown origin/Shard.
  - https://www.17thshard.com/forums/topic/203296-the-sixth-word/ (opened)
  - https://coppermind.net/wiki/Invested_Art (indexed)
- **Scadrian second-skin garment:** the thread reproduces an explicit Scadrial comparison, but the particular garment/magic is unresolved. Do not assert Bands of Mourning, Malwish masks, or aether as the answer.
  - https://www.17thshard.com/forums/topic/203247-the-second-skin-of-scadrial/ (opened)
- **Hoid's memory backups / RoW, WaT and Yumi:** the reference to two previous incidents is discussed, but assigning each incident is disputed and narration-time differs from story-time.
  - https://www.17thshard.com/forums/topic/203168-what-was-the-second-incident-discuss/ (opened)
- **Hoid's Shattering allusion:** chapter XXIII's statement about helping cause a collapse is a candidate, not grounds to declare an additional cosmological catastrophe as fact.
  - https://www.17thshard.com/forums/topic/203134-the-fundamental-collapse-of-all-reality/ (opened)
- **The unidentified dragon helping Vasher:** cooperation is discussed; a particular dragon's identity must not be promoted from theory to confirmed cameo.
  - https://www.17thshard.com/forums/topic/203160-unnamed-character-cooperating-with-a-legendary-figure-discuss/ (indexed)
- **Sunhearts / demonstone; Miral rivers / purified Dor:** useful comparisons in the magitech thread, but similar function does not establish identical origin or technology.
  - https://www.17thshard.com/forums/topic/203105-new-magitech-and-investiture-suply/ (opened)
- **Hoid's audience being Nalthian:** the thread's clothing/color interpretation is explicitly a hypothesis; don't label the audience confirmed Hallandren.
  - https://www.17thshard.com/forums/topic/203095-discuss-fires-of-december-intended-audience/ (opened)
